## About Me

I'm a Master's student in **Internet of Things (IoT)** at **EURECOM**, Sophia Antipolis, France. I'm passionate about building systems where AI meets the physical world — from autonomous robots and smart cities to medical imaging and multi-agent AI.

My work spans the full stack: from low-level embedded C on ARM microcontrollers to training deep learning models on large-scale medical datasets.

🌍 **French · English · Arabic** &nbsp;|&nbsp; 📍 Sophia Antipolis, France

## What I Do

- 🧠 **AI & Deep Learning** — multi-label classification, computer vision, CNN ensembles, explainability
- 🤖 **Embedded Systems & Robotics** — autonomous robot design, FSM architecture, C on ev3dev Linux
- 🔬 **Research** — agentic AI platforms, multi-agent protocols (A2A, MCP, GITER, OpenSlice)
- 🅿️ **IoT Applications** — real-time monitoring, MQTT, Arduino, smart city systems
